## Demand Overflow Analysis

5 buyers were actively enquiring when stock reached zero on 19 March 2026.

These are recorded as Closed Lost opportunities with stage = "Closed Lost — No Stock." They represent:

- **Confirmed demand signal** for a potential restock decision
- **5 customer relationships** that ended without resolution
- **Operational failure** caused by absence of inventory monitoring and waitlist tooling

If a restock had been planned, these 5 contacts would be the first outreach list.

## Notes on Data Quality

All metrics above reflect a manual reconstruction of the original operation. The following limitations apply:

- Response times were not formally recorded; the < 30-minute target is an estimate based on recollection
- Case count (30+) is based on message thread reconstruction; the exact figure may be higher
- CSAT signals were informal (positive marketplace review, WhatsApp thank-you messages); no formal survey was administered
- Health records were created post-pilot for 7 buyers; the remaining 13 buyers have no formal health record

These gaps are acknowledged in the scalability analysis in the main README.
